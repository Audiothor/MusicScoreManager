# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2, 2026*

---

## 🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

**Music Score Manager**, developed by **Audiothor**, is engineered with a strict privacy-first foundation.

### 1. Personal Data Collection & Processing
- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: Music Score Manager does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information or device identifiers (e.g., name, email address, phone number, GPS location, IP address, unique device IDs).
- **No Account Required**: You do not need an account or login credentials to use any feature of the application.
- **100% Local Storage**: All of your sheet music (PDFs, images), audio recordings, annotations, setlists, tags, and backups remain strictly stored on your local device.

### 2. Device Permissions & Purpose
- **Storage / Files & Media**: Used strictly to allow you to select, open, render, and manage your sheet music files and local audio tracks.
- **Wi-Fi Direct & Local P2P Network**: Used exclusively for the optional peer-to-peer (P2P) Wi-Fi Direct and QR Code wireless sharing feature between nearby devices. No data is sent over the internet or to external servers.
- **Location & Nearby Devices**: Required by the Android operating system to detect nearby Wi-Fi Direct devices. The application never accesses, records, or tracks actual GPS coordinates.
- **Camera**: Used strictly and optionally to scan QR codes when joining a local group broadcast.
- **Audio**: Used exclusively to play local audio accompaniment and metronome click/pre-count audio during practice and live performances.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Ads
- **No Advertisements**: The application is 100% ad-free.
- **No Analytics or Trackers**: The application contains no analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.).
