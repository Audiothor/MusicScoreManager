# 🚀 Quick Start Guide (Get Started in 5 Minutes)

Welcome to **Music Score Manager**! This guide walks you step-by-step through setting up, importing your first scores, and being stage-ready for concerts or rehearsals in just a few minutes.

---

## ⚡ At a Glance: The 5 Essential Steps

```
[ 1. Import ] ➔ [ 2. Organize ] ➔ [ 3. View & Play ] ➔ [ 4. Annotate ] ➔ [ 5. Live Setlist ]
    Scores            Tags               Viewer            Pencil/Stickers     Concert Mode
```

---

## Step 1: Import Your First Scores

As soon as the app opens, go to the **Scores** tab (`🎼` icon in the navigation bar).

1. Tap the green **`+ Import`** button at the top right.
2. The file selector opens: select one or more **PDF** or **Image** files (JPEG, PNG).
3. **For PDF files**:
   - The file is immediately added to your library.
   - A modern dialog appears with a dedicated **`🎵 Open score`** button to play right away!
4. **For Photos or Images**:
   - The application suggests automatically converting them to PDF.
   - If you select multiple photos (e.g. photos of individual pages of a piece), the app proposes to **merge them into a single multi-page PDF score**.

> [!TIP]
> **Storage recommendation**: By default, the app copies files into its secure local storage. Your scores remain 100% offline, never dependent on an Internet or Cloud connection.

---

## Step 2: Organize Your Library with Tags (Labels)

To find your pieces instantly among hundreds of scores:

1. In the score list, tap the **three dots `⋮`** on a score, then choose **`Edit`** (or tap the tag below the title).
2. Assign one or more color-coded tags (e.g., *Vocals*, *Piano*, *Jazz*, *Christmas Concert*, *Rehearsal*).
3. You can create custom tags with your own colors via **Settings > Score Settings > Manage Tags**.
4. Use the search bar at the top to filter instantly by **Title**, **Composer**, or by tapping a **Tag**.

---

## Step 3: Play with the Stage Viewer

Simply tap any score in the list to launch the **Full-Screen Stage Viewer**.

- **Page Turning**:
  - **Touch**: Tap the right third of the screen to go forward (next page), left third to go backward (previous page).
  - **Swipe**: Swipe left or right across the screen.
  - **Wireless Foot Pedal**: Pair your Bluetooth pedal (AirTurn, PageFlip, Donner, etc.). Page turning works out-of-the-box without manual mapping!
- **Show / Hide Controls**:
  - Tap the **center** of the screen to smoothly reveal or dismiss the top and bottom toolbars.
- **Stage Lock (`🔒`)**:
  - During live performances, tap the padlock icon to lock the UI and prevent accidental touches while playing.
- **Dark Mode / Inverted Colors (`🌙`)**:
  - Invert colors (white notes on pure black background) for orchestra pits or dim stages without blinding glare.

---

## Step 4: Annotate Your Score (Pencil, Highlighter & Stickers)

In reading mode, tap the center of the screen then the **`✏` (Annotations)** icon:

- **🖌️ Highlighter (Stabilo)**: Highlight bars with translucent colors (yellow, green, pink, blue, orange) with fine or thick strokes.
- **✎ Pencil**: Draw freehand markings, fingerings, or slurs (customizable size and color).
- **T Text**: Insert readable typed notes directly onto the score.
- **❏ Musical Stickers**: Access over 100 categorized symbols (Dynamics *p*, *f*, *crescendo*, breath marks, fingerings, repeats *D.C.*, *Coda*...). Drag & drop them anywhere!
- **↩ Undo / ↪ Redo**: Undo or redo changes at any time.

---

## Step 5: Build a Setlist for a Rehearsal or Concert

Stop searching for your sheets one by one during a performance: prepare your setlist in advance!

1. Go to the **Setlists** tab (`📋` icon).
2. Tap **`+ New Setlist`**, give it a name (e.g. *Concert Dec 15*) and confirm.
3. Tap **`+ Add scores`** to select pieces from your library.
4. Reorder pieces simply by **drag and drop**.
5. Tap **`▶ Start concert`**: the viewer opens on the first score. At the end of the last page, turning the page automatically transitions to the next score!
6. The retractable setlist drawer allows you to see upcoming songs at any moment.

---

## Step 6: Built-in Stage Tools (Metronome & Audio Player)

Directly from the score viewer or via the **Tools** tab:

- **Built-in Metronome (`⏱`)**:
  - Adjust tempo (BPM) with sliders or Tap Tempo.
  - Choose between audible clicks or a flashing visual LED cue (perfect on stage).
- **Backing Track Audio Player (`🎵`)**:
  - Link an audio file (MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC) to a score to practice with the backing track.
  - Control playback (Play / Pause, restart) directly from the viewer.

## 💡 Stage Shortcuts & Best Practices

| Action | Gesture / Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Next Page** | Tap right screen area, Right arrow `→`, Spacebar, or Right Pedal |
| **Previous Page** | Tap left screen area, Left arrow `←`, or Left Pedal |
| **Toggle Menu** | Single tap in the center of the screen |
| **Full-Screen & Keep Awake** | The screen stays awake automatically while viewing a score |
| **Sharing Between Musicians** | Use the **Wi-Fi Direct P2P** menu to send an entire setlist with annotations to fellow musicians backstage, without needing Internet! |
