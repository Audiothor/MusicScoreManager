# 🚀 Quick Start Guide (Get Started in 5 Minutes)

Welcome to **Music Score Manager**! This guide walks you step-by-step through setting up your first scores, organizing your library, and getting stage-ready for concerts or rehearsals in just a few minutes.

---

## ⚡ At a Glance: The 5 Essential Steps

```
[ 1. Import ] ➔ [ 2. Organize ] ➔ [ 3. View & Play ] ➔ [ 4. Annotate ] ➔ [ 5. Live Setlist ]
    Scores            Tags               Viewer            Pencil/Stickers     Concert Mode
```

---

## Step 1: Import Your First Scores

As soon as the app opens, go to the **Scores** tab (`🎼` icon in the navigation bar).

1. Tap the green **`+`** button at the top right.
2. The file picker opens: select one or more **PDF** or **Image** files (JPEG, PNG).
3. **Two import methods are available:**
   - **Copy to application (Recommended Local Mode):** Your scores are duplicated into the app's secure internal storage. This is the recommended choice: your scores remain 100% available offline, even if you move or delete the original files. Music Score Manager easily handles hundreds of scores locally without slowdowns.
   - **Link to source file without copying:** The app references the original file path. This method requires keeping continuous access to the original source file.
4. **For PDF files:**
   - The file is instantly added to your library.
   - A modern dialog appears with a dedicated **`🎵 Open score`** button to play right away!
5. **For Photos or Images:**
   - The application automatically prompts you to convert them to PDF.
   - If you select multiple photos (e.g. photos of consecutive pages of a piece), the app proposes to **merge them into a single multi-page PDF score**.
6. Your scores appear in the list: simply tap any score to open it.

> [!TIP]
> **Storage Recommendation**: Always choose local copy. Your scores stay accessible anytime during rehearsals or gigs without ever relying on an Internet or Cloud connection.

---

## Step 2: Organize Your Library with Tags

To find any song instantly among hundreds of scores:

1. **Create your tags:** Go to **Settings > Score Settings > Organization > Manage Tag List**. Choose a name and custom color (e.g., *Vocals*, *Piano*, *Jazz*, *Christmas Concert*, *Rehearsal*).
2. **Assign tags to a score:**
   - In the **Scores** menu, tap the **three dots `⋮`** on a score card, then choose **`Edit`**.
   - Select one or more tags (a score can have as many tags as needed).
   - Don't forget to save!
3. **Filter in one tap:** Use the top search bar in the library to filter by **Title**, **Composer**, or by tapping one or multiple **Tags** in the filter bar.

---

## Step 3: Use the Stage Score Viewer (Live & Rehearsal)

Simply tap any score in the list to launch the **Full-Screen Stage Viewer**.

- **Turning Pages:**
  - **Touch:** Tap the right third of the screen to turn forward (next page), tap the left third to turn backward (previous page).
  - **Swipe:** Swipe left or right across the screen.
  - **Wireless Foot Pedal:** Pair your Bluetooth pedal (AirTurn, PageFlip, Donner, etc.). Page turning works immediately out of the box with zero manual configuration!
- **Show / Hide Controls:**
  - **Double-click in the center of the screen:** Reveals the quick action menu (zoom, display settings, return to home).
  - **Tap at the bottom of the screen:** Reveals the full annotation toolbar.
- **Integrated Stage Widgets:**
  - **Metronome:** Located top-right (fixed).
  - **Audio Player:** Located top-left (draggable / floating).
  - **Setlist Status Bar:** Located top-center to monitor live song progression.
- **Exit the Score:** Double-click the center of the screen and choose **`Back to Home`**.

---

## Step 4: Annotate Your Score (Pencil, Highlighter, Text & Stickers)

In reading mode, tap the bottom of the screen to open the annotation toolbar:

- **🖌️ Highlighter (Stabilo):** Highlight measures with translucent ink (yellow, green, pink, blue, orange) with fine or thick stroke options.
- **✎ Pencil:** Draw freehand markings, fingerings, or slurs (customizable line width and color).
- **T Text:** Insert neat, legible typed notes directly onto the staff.
- **❏ Musical Stickers:** Access over 100 categorized musical symbols (Dynamics *p*, *f*, *crescendo*, breath marks, fingerings, repeats *D.C.*, *Coda*...). Drag & drop them anywhere or double-tap to place above the staff.
  - *Tip:* In **Settings > Annotation Settings**, you can build your **Favorite Stickers** list for instant access while playing.
- **Delete & Reposition:** Any annotation can be selected, resized, moved, or deleted.
- **↩ Undo / ↪ Redo:** Undo or redo your changes at any time.

> [!TIP]
> **Safety Lock (Prevent accidental marks on stage)**: A padlock icon protects your annotations. When **green**, annotations can be modified. When **red**, the page is locked to prevent any accidental touch inputs while you are performing live.

## Step 5: Build a Setlist for a Rehearsal or Concert

Never scramble for sheet music between songs on stage: prepare your program in advance!

1. Go to the **Setlists** tab (`📋` icon).
2. Tap the **`+`** button at the top right, name your setlist (e.g. *Symphony Hall - Dec 15*), and confirm.
3. Tap **`+ Add`** to select pieces from your library.
4. Reorder songs effortlessly using **drag and drop**.
5. Remember to save your setlist changes.
6. **Launch the concert:** Tap any song in the setlist (or the first one). The viewer opens. Turning past the last page of a score automatically transitions to the next song in the setlist!
7. The **setlist runner drawer** at the top center lets you monitor the current piece and upcoming songs at a glance.

---

## Step 6: Built-in Stage Tools

Directly from the viewer or via the **Tools** tab:

- **⏱️ Built-in Metronome:**
  - Set tempo precisely in BPM or tap the Tap Tempo button.
  - Choose between audible clicks or a flashing silent visual cue (ideal for quiet stages).
- **🎵 Backing Track Audio Player:**
  - Link an audio file (MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC) to a score to practice with backing tracks or demos.
  - Direct Play/Pause and rewind controls without taking your eyes off the sheet music.
- **📡 Wi-Fi Direct P2P Transfer:**
  - Instantly share a score or an entire setlist—including all annotations—with bandmates backstage, tablet-to-tablet, **without requiring an Internet connection or Wi-Fi router**!
- **Duplicate Score Manager:**
  - Automatically detect and clean up duplicate files across large libraries.
- **Backup & Restore:**
  - Back up your entire library, annotations, and setlists into a single portable archive file.
- **PDF Page Assembler & Reorder:**
  - Reorder pages, remove blank sheets, or merge multiple scores into a single custom document.
