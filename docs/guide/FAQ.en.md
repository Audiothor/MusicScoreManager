# FAQ

### Music Score Manager

- *Why develop Music Score Manager?*

There are already very good sheet music viewer applications available on Google Play and the Apple App Store. (Fewer options exist for Windows).
The project was born from a need within music conservatories to offer free software for students and teachers who have a tablet. The project was further encouraged by numerous instrumentalists performing auditions or concerts with local concert bands and community ensembles.
Having initially developed the app for family use, several musicians asked me to make it available to everyone and shared feedback regarding their everyday needs. The application gradually expanded, and in order to benefit as many musicians as possible, I am now officially releasing it.

I am not trying to compete with or replace existing tools, but simply to offer an open, accessible alternative.
The programming language and platform used (.NET C# & .NET MAUI) allow me to make it available for both Android and Windows.

- *Why make it completely free and without ads?*

The target audience includes music lovers, music school teachers, and students—whether performing in live concerts or not, hosting parties, playing at festivals, local music celebrations, weddings, or religious services...
Music Score Manager is here to help. In keeping with the app's philosophy, which originated from a personal family need, it was ethically impossible to monetize or profit from users who do not use it in a commercial context.

- *Why make the source code publicly accessible?*

Although development was done in a language I have used for a long time, I occasionally relied on AI assistance for certain parts of the code (and I make no secret of it).
Because of this, it feels completely natural to remain fully transparent with the codebase and share it freely with everyone on GitHub.

- *What languages does Music Score Manager support?*

French, English, German, Italian, Polish, Spanish, Dutch, and Portuguese.
To change the language, go to the **"Settings"** menu, select **"Application Settings"**, and choose your preferred language.
If you notice any translation inaccuracies, please report them (links below).
If you would like to help contribute other languages, please feel free to reach out (links below).
