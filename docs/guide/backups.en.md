# Backups & Restoration

Ensure your annotations, setlists, and library metadata are always safe and portable.

---

## 💾 Automatic Backups

- The app automatically generates periodic snapshots of your database and settings.
- You can configure the retention history count in the app settings to fit your storage preferences.

---

## 📦 Manual Backup & Export

From the **Tools > Backups** section:
- **Create Backup Now**: Instantly save a timestamped restoration point.
- **Export**: Copy your backup archive to an external USB flash drive, SD card, or cloud folder.

---

## 🔄 Restoration

- When changing devices or rolling back changes, go to **Tools > Backups**.
- Select any previous backup from the list and tap restore. All scores, annotations, and setlists will be restored immediately.
