# PDF Assembler Studio

The PDF Assembler Studio is a comprehensive document workshop built directly into the app, allowing you to edit and restructure sheet music PDFs without third-party software.

---

## 🛠️ Studio Features

- **Convert Images to PDF**: Import several photos or scans (`.jpg`, `.png`), rearrange their order, and compile them into a unified high-resolution multi-page PDF.
- **Rearrange Pages**: Move pages up (▲) or down (▼) with ease.
- **Individual Page Rotations**: Rotate specific pages by 90°, 180°, or 270°.
- **Duplicate or Delete Pages**: Remove blank pages or duplicate repeated movement sections.
- **Reverse Page Order**: Helpful when scans were made in inverted order.

---

## 📍 Accessing the Assembler

1. From the **Tools** tab: Tap **📑 PDF Assembler Studio** to start a new document.
2. Directly from any score: Choose **📑 Modify PDF assembly** from the 3-dots menu or from the viewer's central menu.
