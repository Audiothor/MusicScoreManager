# Wireless Sharing & Transfer

Music Score Manager provides two powerful methods to share sheet music and setlists without needing an internet connection:

---

## 📡 1. Device-to-Device Direct Transfer (Wi-Fi Direct P2P)

Quickly transfer a score or setlist directly between two musicians' tablets or phones.

### Steps:
1. **Receiver**: Turn Wi-Fi on, open the app, go to **Tools > Wi-Fi Direct Transfer**, and tap **Receive**.
2. **Sender**: In the score or setlist options menu, tap **📡 Send via Wi-Fi Direct**.
3. **Choose Options**: The sender selects whether to bundle personal annotations and/or attached audio files.
4. **Send**: Tap the detected recipient device. The transfer completes in seconds over a high-speed local socket.

---

## 📱 2. Group Broadcast via QR Code (Ensemble Sharing)

Distribute a score or a full setlist to an entire band, choir, or orchestra section simultaneously!

### Steps:
1. **The Sender** selects **Group Broadcast (QR Code)**.
2. The app sets up a local Wi-Fi hotspot and displays a **QR Code on screen**.
3. **Ensemble Musicians** scan the QR Code using their camera or enter the local address shown in **Tools > Receive**.
4. The download begins concurrently for all connected group members.
