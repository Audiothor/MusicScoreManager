# 📋 Setlists

The **Setlists** menu is specially designed for planning, organizing, and executing your concert programs, auditions, church services, and rehearsals without downtime.

---

## 📋 Presentation & List Layout

The Setlists tab displays all your created programs:

- **Top Toolbar**: Real-time search, multi-criteria sort (Name A-Z/Z-A, Creation Date, Status), and fast creation button (**➕**) to create a new setlist.

- **Horizontal Status Filters**:
  - *All*
  - *Active* (green badge): programs currently being toured or actively practiced.
  - *Upcoming* (orange badge): planned future concerts.
  - *Completed* (gray badge): archives of past performances.

- **Setlist Cards**:
  - Program title, creation date, status badge, and padlock `🔒` if locked.
  - Swipe gestures for immediate access to *Rename* and *Delete*.
  - Context menu access via the **⋮** button (see below).

---

## ▶️ Instant Stage Start (One-Tap on Card)

In Music Score Manager, going on stage is instantaneous:

- **A simple tap on a setlist card** launches **the first score directly** in the fullscreen stage viewer.
  
- If the setlist is empty (no pieces assigned), an informative dialog alerts you and invites you to add scores to it.
  
- During performance, the setlist activates **continuous reading mode**: when you turn past the final page of a piece, the app automatically transitions to the first page of the next piece in the program!  
  This behavior can also be customized in your setlist options.

---

## 📋 Live Setlist Progress Drawer

**While playing scores from a setlist inside the viewer:**

- **Discreet Top Edge Opening**: A simple tap at the top-center of the screen (semi-transparent touch hotspot) slides down the progress drawer pinned to the top of the display.
  
- **Instant Automatic Centering**: Upon opening, the drawer automatically scrolls to center the currently played song in the list.
  
- **Dedicated Visual Status Palette**:
  - **Currently playing score**: High-contrast midnight blue background, cyan glowing outline, vibrant green `▶` badge, bright white title, and *« Now Playing »* status label.
  - **Passed scores**: Sage green checkmark badge `✓`, muted gray text, and *« Passed »* label.
  - **Upcoming scores**: Pastel blue index number, soft off-white text, composer in azure, and *« Upcoming »* label.
    
- **Navigation & Easy Dismissal**:
  - Tap any piece in the drawer to jump directly and immediately to it.
  - Close the drawer via the `✕` cross button, by tapping the semi-transparent backdrop, or by clicking the current song.

---

## ⋮ Setlist Context Menu (3 dots)

Each setlist provides its own action menu via the **⋮** button:

1. **▶️ Start setlist**:
   - Launches stage mode starting on the first song in full screen.

2. **✏️ Edit setlist**:
   - Opens the editing window:
     * **Title, Status & Concert Date/Time**: Set program title, status badge, and planned performance date.
     * **Padlock Lock**: Locks the setlist into Stage Mode to prevent accidental deletions or modifications during a concert.
     * **Collapsible « Advanced Options » Drawer**:
       - Toggle continuous reading of scores in the specified order.
       - Assign a **dedicated Bluetooth pedal profile** (takes priority during this concert and restores the default profile automatically upon exit).
       - Safely delete the setlist.
     * **Ultra-Fast Score Addition**: Selection modal with instant search, sorting, and **folder/category filter button `[📁]`** to quickly target a repertoire.
     * **Reordering & Management**: Reorder songs using drag-and-drop, duplicate a song, or remove a title with the trash icon.

3. **📡 Send via Wi-Fi Direct**:
   - Transmits the entire setlist and all its score files to other musicians' tablets via direct local streaming without internet.
   - Options dialog:
     * ☑️ *Include handwritten annotations*.
     * ☑️ *Include attached audio backing tracks*.

4. **📦 Export (.msmsetlist)**:
   - Generates a standalone `.msmsetlist` package containing the entire program, performance order metadata, PDFs, and optional annotations and audio tracks.

5. **📑 Duplicate setlist**:
   - Instantly clones the setlist with all pieces preserved in their exact order (ideal for variations of concert programs).

6. **🏷️ Rename**:
   - Fast dialog to modify the title of the setlist.

7. **🗑️ Delete**:
   - Deletes the setlist (your original scores remain completely safe and intact in your library).
