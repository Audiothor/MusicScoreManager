1. Scores Library & Import

The Scores screen is your central sheet music library, storing and managing both PDF documents and Image scores (PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP).

Adding New Scores

1Tap the blue ➕ button
Located at the top right of the Scores screen.
2Pick your files
Your device's file picker opens. Select one or multiple PDF or image score files.
3Import Mode (Copy or Link)
Copy: Copies the file to the app's internal sandbox.
Link (🔗): References the external file without using extra storage space.
⚡
Practical Example: Searching and Filtering

Type keywords in the search bar (e.g. "Chopin" or "Nocturne") for instant filtering across titles, composers, and assigned tags. Use the filter chips [All] [PDF] [Images] to filter by file format.

🏷️
2. Organizing with Tags

Tags let you categorize your music collection by genre, instrument, key, band lineup, or difficulty level.

Managing Tags (Tools Menu)

Manage all your tags in Tools › Tags:

  • Creation: Enter a name and pick a vibrant color from the palette or fine-tune with RGB sliders.
  • Editing & Deletion: Tap any tag to change its label, adjust its color, or remove it.
💡
Musician Tip: 1-Tap Carousel Filter

On the main Scores screen, a horizontal tag carousel displays your colorful tags. Tapping a tag like "Jazz Trio" instantly filters your entire library for that rehearsal!

📄
3. Score Viewer, Navigation & Gestures

The Viewer features a bidirectional RAM pre-rendering pipeline guaranteeing 0 ms latency on forward and backward page turns.

Touch Zones & Configurable Gestures

👉 Next Page Swipe left or tap on the right side (30% of the screen).
👈 Previous Page Swipe right or tap on the left side (30% of the screen).
🔍 Unified Zoom & Pan Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom (1x to 5x), pan with 1 finger to move across the score.
🎯 Quick Central Menu Double-tap in the center of the score to open options, metronome, and audio controls.

Hybrid Page Rotation

Rotate either the current page only (+90°) or the entire score from the central menu.

4. Complete Annotation System

To open the annotation toolbar, tap in the bottom 15% of the screen:

✎ Freehand Pencil 100% opaque drawing. 6 colors, 5 line widths (1 to 5 mm). Perfect for striking out or correcting notes.
🖌 Flat-Tip Highlighter Translucent highlight with flat line caps. 4 fluorescent colors, 3 widths. Never obscures stave lines.
🔤 Interactive Text Tap on the score and type notes/lyrics. 6 colors, 6 sizes (8 to 18 px) with real-time scaling.
🏷 Musical Stickers Dynamics (ff, mp, p), Fingerings (1-5), Breaths, Segno, Coda, Accidentals ready to drag & drop.

Annotation Controls & Safety

  • 🔒 Lock Safety: When locked (red button), annotations cannot be accidentally moved while playing. Unlock (🔓) to edit.
  • ↩ Undo / ↪ Redo: Instantly revert or restore any drawing, highlighting, or sticker placement.
  • Smooth Drag & Move: When unlocked, freely drag any annotation into position without lag or crashes.
  • Quick Delete: Double-tap any drawing, highlight, text, or sticker to remove it.

5. Setlists & Live Concert Preparation

The Setlists section lets you structure your gig order and seamlessly transition between songs on stage.

  • Creation: Tap ➕, enter a title, concert date, and time.
  • Add Scores: Check scores directly from your library to add them to the setlist.
  • Drag & Drop Reordering: Easily rearrange the performance order.
  • Continuous Mode: Turning the last page of a score automatically opens the next song in the setlist!
  • 🔒 Order Lock: Lock the setlist during live performance to prevent accidental edits.
📶
6. Wireless Bluetooth Sharing (P2P)

Share scores and annotations directly between band members without Wi-Fi or Internet access:

1Sender Device
Tap the 3 dots on any score › Exchange (or select multiple scores and tap "Exchange").
2Receiver Device
Navigate to Tools › Bluetooth Transfer › Receive Mode.
3Transfer & Import
The sender taps the target device and transmits the files. Scores and annotations import automatically!

7. Metronome, Audio & Backups

High-Precision Metronome

  • Thread-Safe Regularity: Driven by high-precision hardware Stopwatch timers.
  • Visual LED Beat: Flashing tempo indicator at the top of the score.
  • 880 Hz Pre-Count Beep: Start songs perfectly synchronized with the band.
  • Quick Sound On/Off: Toggle sound in the central menu on the fly.

Automatic & Manual Backups

In Tools › Backup Management, create full SQLite database backups and restore any previous version with a single tap.

8. Settings & Customization
  • Score Settings: Page turn gestures (Swipe/Tap), page number indicator size.
  • Setlist Settings: Default continuous mode and deletion confirmation.
  • Application Settings: Interface language selection (FR, EN, ES, DE) and custom storage directories.
  • Privacy Policy: 100% offline, zero data collection. Accessible under Settings › About.
